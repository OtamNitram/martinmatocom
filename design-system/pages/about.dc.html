How I got here

The short version, including the parts I’m not proud of.

  1. 2008 · Before code

    The long way in

    I fixed PCs, answered phones at a call center, and spent four years in QA before I wrote code for a living.

  2. 2016 · First job as a developer

    Laid off for working alone

    Seven months into my first job as a junior developer, I was at the top of the layoff list. I blamed everyone and everything instead of looking at my part.

    Read the full story →
  3. 2017 · From QA to tech lead

    My first team

    Leading a team for the first time taught me to listen before fixing. For a while, it stuck.

  4. 2018 · A new job

    A company with no bosses

    Here every decision is earned through conversation. I slipped back into knowing it all, until my colleagues were honest enough to tell me.

  5. Today

    Where I am now

    I lead teams for clients in the US, mentor people who are starting to lead, and bring AI into how we work. I still write code when it’s needed. It keeps me close to what the team actually deals with.

How I work

Three ideas I keep coming back to.

  1. 01

    Team building

    Continuous improvement, done by the team itself. I try to build teams that iterate toward quality on their own, not because someone asks them to.

  2. 02

    Psychological safety

    People only try new things, AI included, when they feel safe to get it wrong. The hard part isn’t the tool. It’s adoption, trust, and knowing where to draw the line.

  3. 03

    Ontological coaching

    A human-centered way to grow as a professional. I lean on it when I listen, give feedback or lead.
