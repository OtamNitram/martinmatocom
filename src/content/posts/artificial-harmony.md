I’ve been thinking a lot these days about the concept of “artificial harmony.” Patrick Lencioni describes it brilliantly in Five Dysfunctions of a Team. It happens a lot in our industry: entire teams take refuge in technical urgency to avoid dealing with their dynamics.

> The state a team falls into when it simply lacks the tools to face conflict.
